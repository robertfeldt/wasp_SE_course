\section{Introduction}

This assignment reviews the relationship between software engineering and my research. To provide the necessary context, I will first introduce my research topic.


\paragraph{My Research Topic.}

My research area focuses on the automatic interpretation of football tracking data and the development of representation-learning methods for such data. Football is one of the most widely followed sports worldwide, and the growing availability of advanced tracking technologies has created new opportunities for data-driven performance and tactical analysis. Modern tracking systems use multiple cameras installed around a stadium to record the positions and movements of players and the ball throughout a match. These systems can already capture detailed player skeleton data, providing rich information about body posture and movement. For example, the tracking technology developed by Tracab\footnote{\url{https://www.ea.com/tracab}} enables the large-scale collection of high-quality skeleton data from professional football matches. However, despite the increasing volume and richness of the available data, current methods for automatically interpreting and understanding football tracking data remain limited.


My doctoral research aims to develop AI-based methods for understanding skeleton-based football tracking data. Rather than analyzing players in isolation, my research focuses on interpreting the entire football scene, including the positions, movements, and interactions of all 22 players and the ball over time. In particular, I use machine-learning techniques, including Graph Neural Networks and self-supervised learning, to learn informative representations of player behavior, team interactions, and tactical patterns. These representations can subsequently support applications such as game-event recognition, player-action evaluation, tactical analysis, and team-performance assessment.
